- **Title:** TDLidar Pro Lifetime
- **Length:** Permanent (one-time purchase, no renewal)
- **Price:** US$29.99 (or local-currency equivalent)

### 3.3 What both Pro tiers unlock

HD depth output (1080p / 1440p), edge-preserving smoothing, the six "Pro" colormaps, the camera colormap, head-overlay mode, point-cloud OSC streaming with the on-device 3D viewer, alpha-channel NDI output, raw-mode customization, and PLY export. The free tier of the App remains fully usable without any Pro Feature.
